Technical Details

Compatibility

  • Platform: Android 8.0 (API 26) and above
  • Bluetooth: BLE 4.0+ required
  • Heart Rate Monitors: Any device with standard Heart Rate Service (0x180D)
  • Supported Devices: Polar H10, Garmin HRM-Pro, Wahoo TICKR, and all BLE chest straps

Data & Privacy

  • Storage: All data stored locally on device
  • Export: CSV format with custom date ranges
  • Retention: Configurable (7 days to Forever)
  • Privacy: No cloud sync, no third-party data sharing

Features

  • Recording Modes: Manual toggle and auto-start with workouts
  • Workout Types: Pre-defined + custom interval workouts
  • Alerts: Haptic and sound notifications for zone changes
  • UI Theme: Grafana-inspired dark mode

Advanced Metrics

  • Heart Rate Recovery: Multi-window HRR calculation
  • Chronotropic Competence: Rate of increase + reserve %
  • Zone Analysis: Time and % distribution across zones
  • Trends: Session comparison over 1 week to 1 year

Frequently Asked Questions

Heart Rate Trainer works with any Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) heart rate monitor that uses the standard Heart Rate Service (UUID 0x180D). This includes all major brands: Polar (H10, H9), Garmin (HRM-Pro, HRM-Dual), Wahoo (TICKR, TICKR X), and generic BLE chest straps and armbands.

The app requires a Bluetooth heart rate monitor for live BPM tracking. However, you can use any BLE-compatible device including chest straps, arm bands (like Polar Verity Sense, Wahoo TICKR Fit), or smartwatches that broadcast heart rate data via Bluetooth.

You can set your Max Heart Rate manually based on a recent test, or use the age-based calculation (220 - age). The app automatically calculates 5 zones as percentages of your Max HR. You can adjust your Max HR anytime in Settings or from the Monitor screen.

Yes! In addition to the three pre-defined interval protocols (Modified Tabata, 4x4, Advanced EMOM), you can create custom workouts with multiple steps. Each step can have a custom name, duration, and target heart rate zone.

No. All data is stored locally on your Android device using a local database. Your heart rate recordings, workouts, and settings never leave your phone unless you explicitly export them via the CSV export feature.

Yes! You can export your heart rate data to CSV format for any time range: latest session, last 24 hours, or all recorded data. The exported file can be shared via email, cloud storage, or any app that accepts files.

Heart Rate Recovery measures how quickly your heart rate drops after intense exercise. The app calculates your best HRR drop across multiple time windows (30s, 60s, 120s) and focuses on true recovery phases. Better HRR indicates improved cardiovascular fitness.

Chronotropic Competence measures how effectively your heart rate responds to exercise. The app calculates your best rate of HR increase and provides a reserve-based percentage. This metric helps assess cardiovascular health and training adaptations.

Yes! Heart Rate Trainer works completely offline. You only need Bluetooth connectivity to your heart rate monitor. No internet connection is required for monitoring, recording, or analyzing your workouts.
